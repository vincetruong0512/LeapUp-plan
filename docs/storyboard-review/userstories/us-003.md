# US-003: Approve the storyboard and lock it

## User Story

As a Client, I want to approve the storyboard, so that script, shots and VO are locked for production.

### AC-001 — Storyboard approved and locked

- **Type:** business-rule
- **Covers:** FR-storyboard-review-009 / BR-storyboard-review-001
- **Screen:** storyboard-approved

```gherkin
Given an allowed client and Version 2 ready for approval
When the client presses "Approve storyboard"
Then Version 2 is approved with who and when recorded
And script, shots and VO are locked
```

### AC-002 — Change after lock

- **Type:** business-rule
- **Covers:** FR-storyboard-review-010 / BR-storyboard-review-002
- **Screen:** storyboard-approved
- **Error code:** E-storyboard-review-003

```gherkin
Given Version 2 is approved and locked
When a change to a shot is needed
Then it starts a new revision (Version 3) and Version 2 stays unchanged
```
